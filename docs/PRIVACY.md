# Privacy Policy — GOKO

_Last updated: 2026-06-02_

GOKO ("the app") is an offline-first app for learning and playing the game of Go.
This policy explains what data the app handles.

## What we collect
**GOKO has no servers of its own and does not collect, sell, or transmit your
personal data to us.** We have no analytics SDKs and no advertising.

Data the app stores **only on your device** (via local storage):
- Your learning progress, XP, puzzle/lesson completion, and stars.
- App settings (theme, board preferences, language).
- Local match history of games you play on the device.

Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

## Online play (optional)
If you choose to sign in to **Online-Go.com (OGS)** to play online, that login
and your online games are handled by OGS, a third party, under
**their** privacy policy (https://online-go.com/docs/privacy-policy). Your OGS
authentication token is stored securely on your device (Android Keystore) and is
sent only to OGS to play. GOKO does not receive or store your OGS password.

## Permissions
- **Internet** — used only for optional OGS online play and opening external
  links. All learning and local play work fully offline.

## Children
GOKO does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Local
progress data is stored on-device only.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change accordingly.
